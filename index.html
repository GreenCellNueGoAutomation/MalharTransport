ABOUT US

Trusted Transport & Logistics Partner Across India

Established in 2014, Malhar Transport & Logistics is a trusted logistics and transportation company providing safe, reliable, and timely transport solutions across India. With strong expertise in telecom logistics and a commitment to operational excellence, we ensure secure deliveries through our well-managed network and experienced team. We operate 24×7, 365 days a year, offering dependable daily transportation services tailored to our customers' needs. As we continue to grow, we are expanding into E-commerce and Automobile Logistics, delivering technology-driven solutions with a focus on quality, efficiency, and customer satisfaction. Moving with Trust, Speed & Reliability.
